Staff use this application to keep shared records up to date. Managers use activity and reports to review the work their teams have completed.

**For the commissioning manager.** Start with a reporting period and trace a change back to a representative record. Your application administrator can arrange access to the relevant screens and reports.

**For the IT owner.** Use the system profile to identify the services, data and people needed to keep the application running. Confirm who can approve outages, releases and restores.
