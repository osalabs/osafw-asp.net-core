### Obtain access

Ask the application administrator for the production address and access to the screens your work requires. Give the business reason and the approver. Sign in, complete any MFA enrollment, and confirm that the expected records appear.

### Find, update and verify a record

1. Open the relevant list. Search by a known identifier or name, then check the selected record and any active filters.
2. If you have edit access, change the intended fields and save. Correct any validation messages before leaving the form.
3. Reopen the record and confirm the saved values. For a report, select the relevant dates or filters and compare the result with a known record before distributing it.
