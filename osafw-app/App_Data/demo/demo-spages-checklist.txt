YOUR FIRST WEEK
A simple checklist to discuss with your buddy and team lead.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE
[ ] Confirm your start time and meeting point.
[ ] Share any access needs or helpful adjustments.

DAY ONE
[ ] Meet your buddy and take a workplace tour.
[ ] Check your account and shared tools.
[ ] Agree on your first priority with your team lead.

THIS WEEK
[ ] Meet one person from a team you will work with.
[ ] Complete one small, useful task.
[ ] Keep a list of questions for your next check-in.

FRIDAY CHECK-IN
[ ] What is clearer now?
[ ] Where do you still need help?
[ ] What is the next useful step?

Illustrative sample content. Adapt it to your own workplace.
